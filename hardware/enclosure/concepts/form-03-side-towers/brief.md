# Form 03 — Side Towers, integrated body (refined form study)

Neutral grey clay form study (run the `imagen` skill with `--no-base`). **This pass:
generate multiple ISO variations for review.** Once we pick the best ISO, we iterate to
orthographic front/side/top views on the winner.

**Envelope:** device footprint **≤ 250 × 250 mm — hard maximum, must fit.** Fits the Bambu
X1C 256 mm build plate.

**Dimensional reality (drives the layout):** the 7″ display is ~**194 mm wide** in
landscape — it nearly spans the whole 250 mm width. So the pillars rise at the **front of
the body flanking the central screen/shelf**, and the forward shelf adds **depth**, not
width. (Portrait screen, ~110 mm wide, is the alternative if wider pillars are ever wanted
— deferred.)

**Layout decisions baked in:**
- **One main chunky body at the bottom** — the structural mass, built around the power
  brick (~112 × 76 × 35 mm) and housing the Pi, amplifier and isolator. Sits on the table.
- **Two player pillars are integrated into and rise OUT of this main body** (not separate
  boxes) — sculpted as part of the single form, flanking the central screen. Each pillar:
  a **player-score LED panel near its top** with **headroom above for later adornment**,
  and a **round speaker on its player-facing front face**.
- Central **7″ landscape screen tilted ~65° upward**, rising from the body between the
  pillars.
- **Gems LED panel centered ABOVE the screen** on its own raised centerpiece crest, with
  headroom above for later adornment.
- **RFID card-reader shelf extends out in FRONT of the entire body, onto the desk** — a
  low flat forward-protruding shelf at table level where a card rests/taps.
- **Power cord exits the rear** of the body.
- No OLED, no knob.
